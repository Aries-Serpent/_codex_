## Purpose

These reports document CI/CD pipeline issues, investigations, and resolutions. They serve as:

## Archive Policy

CI/CD reports are moved here once:
1. Issues are fully resolved
2. Fixes are deployed and stable
3. No active work references the reports
4. Content is historical/reference only
