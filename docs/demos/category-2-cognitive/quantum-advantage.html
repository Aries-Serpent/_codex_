What is Quantum Advantage?

The _codex_ cognitive brain achieves a 5.56x quantum advantage over baseline performance, measured by the k₁ constant. This means tasks complete 5.56 times faster than the baseline implementation.

k₁ = Improved Time / Baseline Time

Quantum Advantage = 1 / k₁

Example: If a task takes 100 seconds normally (baseline) and only 18 seconds with optimization (improved), then k₁ = 18/100 = 0.18, and the quantum advantage = 1/0.18 ≈ 5.56x faster! 🚀
