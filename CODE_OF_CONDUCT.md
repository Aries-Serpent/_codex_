# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

**Positive behaviors include:**
- Being respectful and inclusive in language and actions
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Giving credit where credit is due

**Unacceptable behaviors include:**
- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing) without permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders and maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. They Phase 5 take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (GitHub repositories, discussions, pull requests, issues) and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior Phase 5 be reported to:
- **Email**: conduct@aries-serpent.dev
- **GitHub**: Report via repository maintainers (@Aries-Serpent/owners)

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these guidelines in determining consequences:

### 1. Correction
**Impact**: Minor, first-time inappropriate behavior  
**Consequence**: Private written warning with clarity on violation and explanation of why behavior was inappropriate

### 2. Warning
**Impact**: Violation through a single incident or series of actions  
**Consequence**: Warning with consequences for continued behavior; no interaction with involved parties for specified period

### 3. Temporary Ban
**Impact**: Serious violation or sustained inappropriate behavior  
**Consequence**: Temporary ban from community interaction for specified period

### 4. Permanent Ban
**Impact**: Pattern of violations, sustained harassment, or aggression  
**Consequence**: Permanent ban from community interaction
