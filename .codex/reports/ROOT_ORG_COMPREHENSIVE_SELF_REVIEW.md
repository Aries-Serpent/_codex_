### Best Practices Identified

1. **Always validate before moving** (even "safe" files)
2. **Create navigation immediately after organizing**
3. **Use git mv for atomic operations**
4. **Log everything to NDJSON for audit trail**
5. **Test with --dry-run before production**
6. **Commit in small batches** (10-20 files optimal)
7. **Document decisions comprehensively**
8. **Build reusable infrastructure** (don't one-off)
