It contains two distinct recursive calls that lack depth checks: Line 786: Recursively decodes components of `SEQUENCE` or `SET` types. Line 907: Recursively decodes elements of `SEQUENCE OF` or `SET OF` types.  4. `_decodeComponentsSchemaless` (Line 661): ```for component in decodeFun(substrate, **options):``` This method handles decoding when no schema is provided.
